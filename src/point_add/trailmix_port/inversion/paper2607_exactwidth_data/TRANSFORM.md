# Q816 Aux4 certified primitive stream

These 36 contiguous shards are the fixed-point-reduced primitive stream for
all 1,616 steps of the four-clean-auxiliary paper2607 schedule. Each sidecar
records its exact compressed hash, raw-record hash, primitive histogram, and
per-step totals. `aggregate.json`, `aggregate_manifest.json`, and
`reduction_manifest.json` bind the complete traversal and reduction receipts.

The source was generated by `generate_q816_final_stream.py`, reduced by
`reduce_q816_stream.py`, and independently checked by `verify_q816_stream.py`.
The embedded local layout has width 570: a 560-qubit persistent EEA core plus
ten restored dirty references supplied by the surrounding point-add circuit.
With the 256-qubit external point lane, the measured challenge peak is 816.

The reduced stream contains 221,232,963 records per traversal, including
170,420,517 CCX records and 6,448 clean-C3X MBU markers. Its executed cost is
170,433,413 Toffolis per traversal. The four EEA traversals therefore contribute
681,733,652 Toffolis before the unchanged surrounding point-add operations.

The stream is scoped to the open public ECDSA Fail reversible point-addition
benchmark and does not by itself replace the required trusted 9,024-shot replay.
