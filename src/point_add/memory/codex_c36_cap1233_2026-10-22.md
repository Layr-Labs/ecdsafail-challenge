# C36: 1,233-qubit experiment (2026-10-22)

Accepted baseline at `3eefcf21`:

- peak width: 1,234 qubits
- emitted operations: 11,682,054
- average executed Toffolis: 755,313.657
- score proxy: 932,057,052.738
- 9,024/9,024 official shots pass with `TAIL_NONCE=1052265542902`

## Construction

`leapfrog::C36` is a compile-time research switch and is **off by default**.
When enabled it sets both walk and square caps to 1,233 and makes three exact
terminal one-fold scheduling changes:

1. Extend the forward carry loan to tick 128.  This is cheaper than dropping
   and rebuilding a kept carry at that tick.
2. Drop one kept carry at reverse tick 125 (tick 126 already drops one).
3. Allow the existing deep shed to compose with the dropped-carry ladder at
   forward tick 130 and reverse tick 126.

The source-bound edit tables are hashes of the complete 1,234-qubit stream and
cannot be reused.  Under the C36 switch, `stream_edits` therefore runs only the
stream-derived exact passes.  A final `classical_wide` product pass
finds one further exact CCX weakening for C36.

## Best observed C36-v2 stream

- peak width: 1,233 qubits
- emitted operations: 11,705,323
- builder expected Toffolis before the final stream passes: 755,077.2
- average executed Toffolis on the accepted baseline's 9,024-shot corpus:
  756,225.898
- classical mismatches: 0
- phase-garbage batches: 0
- ancilla-garbage batches: 0
- score proxy: 932,426,532.234

The cross-seeded run is a semantic A/B test, not an official challenge pass:
the candidate was executed using the accepted baseline stream's Fiat-Shamir
input and simulator-randomness stream.  A temporary evaluator hook used for
that comparison was removed afterwards.

C36 is therefore a valid width-reduction construction on the tested corpus,
but it is not yet a score improvement.  At 1,233 qubits the break-even average
is 755,926.239 Toffolis; C36-v2 is 299.659 above it (about 0.03964% worse in
score).

## Negative result: rebasing old source edits

Blindly relocating the old `and_cache.txt`, `product_copy.txt`, and
`value_edits.txt` records by matching their local input operations is unsafe.
That experiment produced classical failures on every shot.  The edits form
coordinated cache lifetimes and refer to stream-specific free wires; local
operation equality does not prove the replacement scratch wire is free or
that every build/use/uncompute site has moved together.

## Next target

The difference between the C36 stream-derived cost and the executed baseline
indicates roughly 140 average Toffolis of loss from skipping the old
source-bound cache package.  Rebuild that package from liveness/value proofs
for the C36 stream, rather than rebasing indices.  Even after recovering it,
about 160 more average Toffolis of cap-1,233-specific per-cell retuning are
needed to beat the current score.  Any changed final stream also needs a fresh
Fiat-Shamir nonce; the accepted baseline nonce is not transferable.
