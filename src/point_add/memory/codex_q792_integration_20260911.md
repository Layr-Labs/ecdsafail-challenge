## Reversible encoding and arithmetic

Twenty metadata wires encode rank and low A/C/S fields. LOW, reflected EDGE
and PEAK charts have explicit inverse mappings and a disjoint terminal marker
47 with eight history bits. A temporary 21st rank wire is leased from a phase
bit whose passenger is preserved by the surrounding physical protocol.
The global A-addressed zero pad loan survives the entire step. S0 exit moves
its passenger to the new A pad through the existing SM3 lease before A changes.
R00, entry, newborn, Sign, terminal-history and final cargo use the same20-bit
physical state. No operation may touch omitted Work1 positions256,257,258.
The full circuit peak was measured at792, separately from these local facts.

R01 changes to a sum-major six-bit chart during C+=A, exposing M=A+C high
bits and recovering S high bits from the remaining code. Small-S seed and
low updates temporarily expose numeric A/C; all reversible extensions are
undone around their guarded centers. T10 uses numeric rank4 for C1 or smallS;
its general branch and R01 use an existing guard as a temporary high-predicate
cache, with exact inverse mask computation. The Sign path does not consume
its leased P2 rank rail as an independent carry bit.

## Exact operation reductions

Four-variable ESOP plans are generated by generate_esop4_q792_r01.cpp.
The checked table is q792_esop4_r01.bin, used by q792_esop_r01.rs. Every truth
vector selected by synthesis is evaluated exhaustively. Five/six-variable
Davio/Shannon compositions and bounded deterministic polarity descent for
larger data predicates choose shorter emitted expressions and preserve the
same Boolean function. No stochastic circuit seed or benchmark case search
is used. Consecutive counter transpositions share a common affine frame only
when their exact disjoint fixed-base cosets and XOR displacement agree.

Identical predicate writes to several targets are conjugated by CX fanout:
pre/post CX(t0,ti) surrounding t0 ^= f yields ti ^= f for every target. The
controls are disjoint from every fanout target, all targets may be arbitrary,
and no extra clean wire is required. The four chart families use this exact
identity. Guarded peak conversion is a nine-bit partial permutation plus a
separate terminal transposition, preserving A0 and isolating the A1 marker.
